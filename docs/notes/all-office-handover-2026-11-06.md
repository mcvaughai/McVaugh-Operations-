## Things stated here for the first time

**GL insurance has a cadence.** Check the spreadsheet **every other week** and set a reminder. Yellow means
within 30 days of expiring. Send one email to everyone who is yellow. Some agencies send renewals
automatically, some two weeks out, some on the day. The point is "don't pay them without insurance."
*Why it matters:* MCH carries builder's risk only, which covers the house. It does not cover anyone
working on it. If a framer falls off a roof, his own workers' comp and general liability are what respond.

**Budget increases now need two approvals.** A standard budget goes to the Director of Operations alone.
An increase needs the Director of Operations **and** Jim, because Teresa is now Director of Operations.
And the approver gets no notification: "the only way I know there's a budget change needing approval is
because you have to tell me." So email Teresa and Kenneth after you submit.

**The purchasing mailbox gets added to both James and Brittany.** Insurance certificates should be sent
there rather than to a personal inbox. James should send everything purchasing from it.

**Maria's own estimate of the workload she is handing James: about an hour and a half a week.** "Nothing
you are about to assign James is a full-time job."

**How far the permit documents go.** She could not document past the point where a portal opens, because
no application is open right now. The city only opens the second portal once the full application and
address verification are done. That second portal is step by step and tells you to call the city with
questions. Everything City of Houston is paid through a shopping cart, and the cart number arrives by
email from whichever department wants paying.
