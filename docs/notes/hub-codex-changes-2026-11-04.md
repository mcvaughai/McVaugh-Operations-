## Rewritten: three procedures, and this needs your judgement

These were not trimmed. Every step was replaced with denser wording.

| Procedure | Steps before | Steps after |
|---|---|---|
| Work Order Entry in BuildConnect (SOP-PUR-01) | 15 | 11 |
| Material Purchase Order Entry (SOP-PUR-02) | 15 | 8 |
| Permitting — iPermits / ProjectDox (Current) | 14 | 7 |

**What the rewrites gained.** Vendor rules now sit inline where they are used: Johnston at 409 with
separate priced scopes, HVAC at 60/30/10 following WO 39974, H&H HOA fence at 940. They also add honest
caveats the old versions lacked, such as the ten-minute turnaround being "discussed, not a universal SLA",
and the water-heater single draw being "a historical example, not every case".

**What the rewrites lost.** Mechanics. The old versions told you which button to press; the new ones
assume you already know.

- **Work order entry** dropped the navigation (`Jobs → routed job → New Work Order`), the warning that
  calendar selections have sometimes failed to persist, and "never create a duplicate" as its own step.
- **Material PO** lost the most: selecting `Jobs → New Purchase Order`, separating merchandise from tax,
  verifying the form recalculates after blur, and the distinction between Complete, Received, Pay and
  Paid In Full. Eight steps will not get someone through their first PO.
- **Permitting** is the biggest loss. The old version was a how-to: log into iPermits for the
  jurisdiction, create the application, upload plans and reports, pay the fees, track status, answer
  ProjectDox comments, schedule inspections, file the approved permit. The new version is mostly things
  to verify and confirm. It reads as an audit checklist rather than a procedure.

**My recommendation: merge rather than choose.** Keep the old step-by-step as the body and fold the new
caveats and vendor rules into it. Permitting especially — Lonnie takes it over and Maria's last day was
6 November, so a checklist of things to confirm is the wrong artefact for him right now.
