## Work Order Entry in BuildConnect (SOP-PUR-01)

**Owner:** james · **Backup:** ernesto

1. Read the source estimate or invoice and extract vendor, verified job and routing, reference number, date, scope, quantities, rates, total, work site, schedule and approval context. Check it against the current approved quote: selections, plans, takeoffs, counts and inclusions. A source document on its own is not spending approval.

2. Apply routing first. HOA work requested and approved through Teresa goes to ROCVTA: keep ROCVTA as the database job and put the full physical work address prominently in the description. Do not substitute the bill-to address.

3. In logged-in Chrome, check Work Order Tracking for the selected job before entry and before every retry. Use All and include voided records and related extras, so a duplicate cannot be created.

4. Open Jobs, then the correctly routed job, then New Work Order in the BuildConnect frame.

5. Set the payment-period count FIRST. Changing it reloads the form, so anything entered before it is lost.

6. Apply the current vendor and scope rule:
   · Johnston, membrane and built-up roofing, code 409. Separate priced scopes; leave the general square footage and rate blank; put the supplied area and rate in the scope row; preserve the package totals.
   · All A/C work orders: 60/30/10, following the exact draw descriptions on WO 39974. Recalculate the amounts from each new contract total and reconcile the rounding. Pricing is equipment, register and system based, never per square foot. Enter the verified A/C area in the square-footage field AND in the saved description. The 2,626 A/C square feet applies to 11603 Royal Parkside only.
   · H&H HOA fence maintenance: ROCVTA, Maintenance, code 940, one 100% draw for one priced scope. Other HOA trades need their coding verified first.
   · Synergy roofing: 30/50/20 draws.
   · Electrical Works change orders: code 620.
   · Reece plumbing fixtures: code 601; split valves and trims when asked.

7. Verify the line amounts after blur. Confirm the sum matches the contract total and the percentages come to 100.

8. Use the authorized schedule. The invoice or estimate date is NOT automatically the start date. Put the proposal date in the applicable Invoice Date fields for proposal orders, and the actual invoice date for invoices. Start and completion are schedule dates with an agreed duration, separate from the invoice date. Leave the completion and payment flags unset on new work.

9. Respect any requested review stop. Submit only within current authorization, using the passcode-box Enter step, and keep the passcode private. Do not sign for a vendor, and do not infer field acceptance, completion or payment approval.

10. A blank or white screen after submitting is an uncertain result, not proof of failure, and may well mean it saved. Do not retry immediately.

11. Reopen Work Order Tracking and the saved record. Verify the work-order ID, routed job, vendor, reference, rows, labelled area, dates and total.

12. If verification is inconclusive, stop and report the uncertainty. Never create a duplicate.

13. Calendar selections have sometimes failed to persist. If a date does not take, report the requested date against the saved one and preserve the existing record. Date persistence, edit permissions and the WO 40186 correction are all still open.

14. Once the saved order is verified, file the original proposal, estimate or invoice in the existing Public, matching job, Purchasing, accepted-bid-work-order, vendor folder structure. Reuse the structure that is actually there, create only a missing vendor folder, preserve the original and verify the copy. H&H HOA source documents go in community Purchasing, ROCV H&H Fence.

15. An urgent request for a ready work order was expected promptly, about ten minutes in discussion, but that was never agreed as a standing commitment. Confirm who is primary and backup, and the supplier deadline, rather than assuming.

## Material Purchase Order Entry (SOP-PUR-02)

**Owner:** james · **Backup:** ernesto

1. Obtain the approved quote, proposal or invoice. Compare it against the approved selections, specification, plans and takeoff: vendor, job address, items, quantities, inclusions and pricing. Confirm the price, the actual tax, any deposit, delivery, the source date and reference, and the approval. Flag any discrepancy before going further.

2. Verify the job address and match it to the correct BuildConnect job. Use the job and vendor that already exist rather than creating new ones.

3. Check Purchase Order Tracking for duplicates before entry and before every retry, comparing reference, date, scope and amount.

4. Select Jobs, then the correct job, then New Purchase Order.

5. Select the vendor and the cost code for the material type: 601 for plumbing fixtures, 996 for Lowe’s and Home Depot.

6. Set the payment rows. Split into separate draws when the scope calls for it, such as Reece valves against trims when that is requested. Use a single draw for a standalone quote. The water-heater single draw is one historical example, not the default for every quote.

7. Enter the amounts. Separate merchandise from the actual tax where that applies, and check the form recalculates after blur.

8. Enter the invoice or proposal number and the real date: the proposal date for proposal-based orders, the invoice date for invoices.

9. Submit, then verify the saved purchase order in Tracking: PO number, job, vendor, reference, amounts, dates and the cost code.

10. For a supplier-dispatched PO, email the authorized order to the verified contact on the proposal and keep both the dispatch and the supplier’s acknowledgment. Check who the current recipients are rather than copying Maria out of habit. A work-order portal notification does not establish that a PO was delivered.

11. Mark Complete only when authorized. Complete is a separate thing from Received, Pay and Paid In Full. Lowe’s receipt status, Canon and the physical handoff follow their own procedure: do not apply the Lowe’s complete-for-payment rule to every material PO.

12. File the unchanged source document and the PO PDF where applicable, in the existing Public, matching job, Purchasing, accepted-bid, vendor folder structure. Preserve the originals and verify the copy.

13. Extra lumber ordered before 9 AM may catch the same-day truck. Verify the supplier cutoff and the early coverage, and never promise a truck without confirming it. Expedited entry for early lumber or material coverage is job-specific, not a standing commitment.
